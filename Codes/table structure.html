About the Periodic Table

The Periodic Table is a tabular display of all known chemical elements, arranged by increasing atomic number, electron configuration, and recurring chemical properties.

History

First organized in 1869 by Russian chemist Dmitri Mendeleev, who arranged elements by atomic mass and predicted new ones.

Structure

Elements are arranged in rows (periods) and columns (groups) based on similar properties. Metals are on the left, non-metals on the right.

Fun Fact

Only 94 elements occur naturally. The rest are synthetic and made in laboratories!
