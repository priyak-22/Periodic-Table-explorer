Electronic Configuration & Orbitals

Electronic configuration describes how electrons are arranged in an atom's orbitals. This determines an element's chemical behavior and position in the periodic table.

Rules for Filling Orbitals

  • Aufbau Principle: Electrons occupy the lowest energy orbitals first.

  • Pauli Exclusion Principle: No two electrons can have the same set of quantum numbers.

  • Hund's Rule: Electrons occupy orbitals singly before pairing up.

Examples of Configurations

  • Hydrogen (1): 1s¹
  • Carbon (6): 1s² 2s² 2p²
  • Iron (26): [Ar] 4s² 3d⁶
  • Uranium (92): [Rn] 7s² 5f⁴ 6d¹

Subshells and Orbitals

Subshells are divisions of electron shells, each containing orbitals where electrons reside. The main subshells are s, p, d, and f.
