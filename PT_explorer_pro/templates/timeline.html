Discovery Timeline of Periodic Table Blocks

1735 - 1807

s-block

Elements like Hydrogen, Lithium, and Sodium were among the earliest discovered due to their high reactivity and abundance.

1808 - 1900

p-block

Non-metals and noble gases like Oxygen, Nitrogen, and Neon were isolated and identified during this time.

1790s - 1920s

d-block

Transition metals like Iron, Copper, and Zinc were known since ancient times, but formally studied during this period.

1900 - 1940+

f-block

Lanthanides and actinides like Uranium and Plutonium were discovered during the 20th century, often with complex isolation processes.
